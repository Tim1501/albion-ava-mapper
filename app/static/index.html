Home / Overlay

Overlay

The strip that sits in the black bar above or below the game. It only displays; everything you can do with it is here (and in the tray icon's menu).

Showing now

What it read

Controls

Reset puts the strip back in the middle of the bottom black bar.

Overlay size

Big fits the black bars around an ultrawide game. On a normal screen without bars, pick Medium or Small: they only show the important bits, so they can sit over a corner of the game all the time.

Hotkeys

Hover a portal on the map and press the read hotkey: Albion Ava Mapper reads the tooltip around your mouse (like a screenshot, it never touches the game) and shows that zone and the portal's closing time. The move hotkey unlocks the strip so you can drag it; press it again to lock it.

Home / Report a bug

Report a bug

Something not working? Make a bug report and send it to me on Discord. It's one small zip file.

What happened?

What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. English or not, anything helps.

How to send it

  1. Write what happened in the box, then press Create bug report.
  2. The report is saved on your Desktop as Albion Ava Mapper bug report <date>.zip, and the folder opens with the file selected. (If there's no Desktop folder, it goes into the app's data folder.)
  3. Open Discord and send a message to (click to copy the name).
  4. Drag the zip into the chat, add a line about what went wrong, and send it.

What's inside

  • What you wrote
  • The app's log, settings and version, your Windows version and screen size
  • The text from the last F8 read, and (if ticked) its picture

Nothing else: no passwords, no game account details. You can open the zip and check it before sending.

Home / Data

Data

Where the zone information comes from, and how to refresh it.

Zone data

Built from Albion's game files: the world list (zone names, types, resources, minimap markers), each zone's layout, and the camps it places. Chests are counted from the chest spawn points in each camp for the zone's tier. Update after an Albion patch.
