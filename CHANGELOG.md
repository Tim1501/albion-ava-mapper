# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First public release
- Zone finder for all 400 Roads of Avalon zones: chests (green, blue, gold, small/big), gathering spots, dungeons and where each zone connects, all from Albion's own game files
- F8 reads the portal tooltip under the mouse and shows the zone and its closing timer in a compact overlay
- Overlay in three sizes: Big for the black bars around a 21:9 game, Medium and Small for normal screens (only the important bits)
- The overlay is click-through, stays off the taskbar and shows up in screenshots
- Minimize to tray, Discord copy text, saved zones, sorting and filters
- Donate button: any support is appreciated
